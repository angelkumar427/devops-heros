################ 4. Filter by priority: errors and worse ################
# scoped to one unit, which is how you would actually use it when
# investigating a specific service rather than the whole system:
$ journalctl -p warning -b -u docker.service --no-pager | tail -8
-- No entries --

$ journalctl -p err -b -u docker.service --no-pager | tail -4
-- No entries --
(no output above = this service logged nothing at err or worse this boot)
